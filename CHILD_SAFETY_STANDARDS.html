Child Safety Standards

Eyes On Me  |  Last updated: February 25, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Eyes On Me is firmly committed to protecting children from sexual abuse, exploitation, and any form of harmful content. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) content on our platform.

2. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on Eyes On Me:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses minors (CSAM).
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behavior targeting minors.
  • Content that sexualizes individuals under the age of 18.
  • Any communication or material that endangers the safety of children.

3. Detection & Prevention

  • All user-generated content is subject to automated and manual moderation.
  • Accounts found violating child safety policies are permanently banned.
  • We use industry-standard tools to detect and remove prohibited content.

4. Reporting

  • Users can report harmful or abusive content directly within the app at any time.
  • All reports related to child safety are treated with highest priority and reviewed promptly.
  • Confirmed CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline, as required by U.S. federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and to relevant local and national authorities as appropriate.

5. Compliance

Eyes On Me complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to the PROTECT Our Children Act and the laws of Turkey (Law No. 5651 and related regulations). We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies upon receipt of valid legal requests.

6. Age Restrictions

Eyes On Me is intended for users aged 13 and above. Users under 13 are not permitted to create accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
